Frequently asked

How often do you publish?

News runs throughout the day during tournament weeks. The daily brief goes out every morning at 6 AM, year-round.

Is the newsletter really free?

Yes. The daily brief and all news coverage are free. A paid tier adds the full analysis archive and subscriber-only interviews.

Which tours do you cover?

Both main tours plus Challenger, ITF, and junior events — including the qualifying rounds that rarely get broadcast.

Can I pitch a story or send a tip?

Please do. Tips go to tips@deuce.example, and freelance pitches to editorial@deuce.example. We read everything.

Do you take advertising from brands?

We run limited sponsorships, always labeled. Sponsors get no input on coverage, and editorial never sees the deal terms.
