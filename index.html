Frequently asked

How long does the battery last?

About six hours of continuous feeding, or roughly four full sessions. It charges to 80% in ninety minutes.

How many balls does the hopper hold?

250 balls. At a standard two-second interval that's a little over eight minutes of uninterrupted feeding.

Does it work on any court surface?

Yes — hard, clay, and grass. The drive wheels adjust feed angle to account for how the surface plays.

What's included in the warranty?

Three years covering motors, battery, and electronics, plus free return shipping during the 60-day trial.

Can I use it without the app?

Yes. Onboard controls run every preset drill. The app and watch integration are optional.
