About the research

This is a market-research survey. We're asking the tennis community about their needs, preferences, and experiences in the game.

  • Who it's for

    Competitive players, parents of junior players, and coaches. Each group answers questions relevant to their role.

  • What we ask

    Questions about how you train, compete, and make decisions around the sport.

  • How responses are used

    Responses inform our research into the tennis community. We are not selling anything through this survey.
